Proposed research lab

ASTRA Lab

Advanced Systems for Temporal, Multimodal, and Space Research.

ASTRA Lab is the research group I plan to establish as an Assistant Professor. It will develop artificial intelligence systems for temporal data, multimodal learning, scientific data generation, and space-weather prediction.
